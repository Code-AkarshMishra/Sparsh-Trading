1. How to Browse Products & Services

  1. Products Catalogue (/products): View Tata Steel Chaukhat, PPGI frames, uPVC windows, frameless glass railings, CNC main entrance gates, and modular kitchens.
  2. Interactive Before & After Slider: Visit the homepage to compare old wooden frames and iron grills with Sparsh engineered products.
  3. Regional City Pages (/locations/pratapgarh, /locations/sultanpur, etc.): Check regional installation teams and local projects in your district.
  4. Multilingual Translation: Click the globe icon (🌐) in the top navigation bar to switch between 22 Indian languages.

2. How to Place a New Order or Request Quotation

STEP 1 Create an Account or Login: Click Register (or Login with your user icon) in the header using your name, phone number, and password.

STEP 2 Navigate to "Place New Order": Open /dashboard/new-order from your customer portal sidebar or mobile header.

STEP 3 Fill in Project Details: Select your product type, enter dimensions (e.g. 7x3.5 ft, 8 frames), and specify delivery location.

STEP 4 Instant WhatsApp Dispatch: Submit the form and click "Send via WhatsApp" for instant priority booking.

3. Live Order Tracking & Milestones

Your custom order goes through verified workshop quality stages:

Stage What Happens at Our Workshop Customer Notification
1. Measurement Technical team visits your construction site with digital laser tools. SMS / WhatsApp visit confirmation
2. 3D CAD Drawing AutoCAD cut-sheet blueprint generated to exact millimeters. Design approval shared
3. Fabrication Hydraulic press bending, TIG/MIG welding, and frame mortising. Live production WhatsApp update
4. Anti-Rust Coating Zinc chromate primer and baked electrostatic finish applied. Finishing notification
5. Installation Technicians arrive on-site with anchor bolts for neat fitting. Dispatch & fitting team alert

4. Sharing Designs with Architects & Contractors

Every product detail page features a green "📲 Share on WhatsApp" button. Click it to immediately forward the high-resolution CAD photos and structural specifications to your building contractor or family members.
