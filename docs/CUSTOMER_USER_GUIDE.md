## 2. 🛒 How to Place an Order
1. **Register / Login:** Click **Register** in the header and create an account with your mobile number.
2. **Open Order Form (`/dashboard/new-order`):** Select your product category and enter approximate dimensions.
3. **Instant WhatsApp Confirmation:** Click **Send via WhatsApp** to immediately share your requirements with our fabrication team.

---

## 3. ⏱️ Live Order Milestones
| Stage | Workshop Process |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Measurement** | On-site laser measurement by technician |
| **2. 3D CAD Drawing** | Computerized AutoCAD cut-sheet generation |
| **3. Fabrication** | Hydraulic press bending, welding & frame mortising |
| **4. Finishing** | Zinc anti-rust primer and electrostatic paint coating |
| **5. Installation** | Delivery and on-site fitting with anchor fasteners |
