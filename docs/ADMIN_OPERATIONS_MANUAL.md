# 🛠️ Sparsh Trading — Admin & Operations Manual

This guide covers daily administrative workflows, lead handling, production stage management, content publishing, and verified media deletion.

---

## 🏛️ Admin Navigation Structure
- **Dashboard (`/admin`):** Real-time key metrics and operational overview.
- **Enquiries (`/admin/enquiries`):** Customer inquiries with dimensions, location, and preferred contact mode.
- **Orders (`/admin/orders`):** Milestone progression and 1-click WhatsApp customer status notifications.
- **Content Hub (`/admin/content`):** Product, project, and gallery uploads with 3-step verified asset deletion.
- **Invoices (`/admin/invoices`):** Real GST invoices and commercial billing.
- **Team (`/admin/team`):** Internal leadership directory and role management.

---

## 💬 1-Click WhatsApp Live Status Dispatch
1. Navigate to `/admin/orders`.
2. Find the active customer order.
3. Click **"💬 Send WhatsApp Update"**.
4. A pre-formatted, professional milestone update will be ready to send to the client.

---

## 🛡️ 3-Step Verification Media Deletion
1. **Step 1 — Preview:** Review thumbnail image and metadata.
2. **Step 2 — Risk Check:** Check the acknowledgment checkbox.
3. **Step 3 — Security Keyphrase:** Type `DELETE` in capital letters to authorize permanent erasure.
